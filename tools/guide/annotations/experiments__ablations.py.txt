== """Change one thing
An *ablation* removes or changes one part and measures what was lost.

== def job
`believed` is the physics the loss is told, and `osc` is the physics that made the dots. Keeping them apart is what makes the wrong-physics experiment possible.

== def variants
A table of settings. Each entry is a label, the options for `train_pinn`, the noise and the believed physics.

== if __name__
Three seeds per setting. That is enough to see large effects and too few to rank close ones.
